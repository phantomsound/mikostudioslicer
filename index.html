MikoStudioSlicer - Production Workflow Guide

How to take flat AI concepts apart into genuine Photoshop layers and vector paths

1 Smart Box Extraction

Drag a box over any logo, badge, or banner. The app crops the region and defringes edges. Click AI Defringe on any layer card to trigger the local neural engine.

2 Color Plate Deconstruction

Switch to 2. Color Plate Deconstruct. It separates the artwork into its 6 core brand inks (Gold, Cyan, Violet, Crimson, Obsidian, White) without hollowing out letter centers.

3 Live Composer Stage

In 3. Live Composer, layers float independently. Drag to reposition, toggle visibility, and test layout balance for broadcast overlays before export.

4 Export to Photoshop & Vectors

Click Export Layered .PSD to create an authentic multi-layer Photoshop document. Each graphic sits on an editable floating layer.
